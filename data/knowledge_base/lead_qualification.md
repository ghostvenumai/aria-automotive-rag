# Sales Lead Qualification

Inbound leads should be classified by purchase horizon, preferred body style, financing interest and whether the customer wants to trade in an existing vehicle. A high-intent lead usually mentions a model family, budget expectations or a concrete delivery window.

The assistant may answer general inventory and financing process questions, but it should avoid inventing stock numbers or rebate promises. When the request becomes deal-specific, such as asking for an exact lease offer or approval status, the assistant should recommend a human sales specialist.

Confidence should be lower when the customer request mixes sales, service and complaint topics in a single message because the workflow may require multiple departments.
