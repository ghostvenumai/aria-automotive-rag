# Vehicle Handover Assistant

The dealership handover flow should confirm the delivery date, model, trim and customer identity before the vehicle leaves the showroom. Sales staff should explain financing milestones, warranty coverage, service booking channels and the digital driver app during the handover appointment.

When a customer asks for a delivery status update, the assistant should first clarify whether the vehicle is in production, in transit or already prepared for pickup. If a promised date is at risk, the assistant should recommend a human escalation to the delivery coordinator instead of giving speculative timelines.

Common questions include registration paperwork, first service interval, charging guidance for EV customers and ownership transfer steps for trade-in scenarios.
