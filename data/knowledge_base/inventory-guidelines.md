# Inventory Guidelines

High-demand SUVs and hybrid models are refreshed in the internal stock feed several times per day. Sales assistants should avoid confirming an exact unit until the stock feed and reservation state have both been checked. Test-drive-ready vehicles are marked separately from inbound stock.

If a customer asks for color, trim, or battery-range details, the assistant should answer only when the requested detail is present in the structured inventory feed or in an approved product brief.
