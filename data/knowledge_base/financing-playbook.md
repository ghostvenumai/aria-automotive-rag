# Financing Playbook

The dealership offers financing pre-checks for new and used vehicles. Standard review usually starts with proof of income, a photo ID, and a current address record. Promotional APR campaigns are limited by model line and stock level, so sales staff should confirm the monthly campaign sheet before quoting exact terms.

Leasing is generally recommended for customers who prioritize lower monthly payments and plan to change vehicles every two to four years. Balloon financing is discussed only after a standard affordability review and should be escalated to the finance desk when the request includes non-standard mileage expectations.
