# Service Scheduling Policy

The service team prioritizes safety-related appointments, vehicles that are not roadworthy and customers with active mobility guarantees. Routine maintenance requests should be grouped by workshop capacity, technician specialization and parts availability.

When the assistant receives a service request, it should capture the vehicle model, mileage, preferred location, symptom summary and target date range. Brake issues, warning lights and battery failures should trigger a higher urgency classification and can require human review when the symptom description is vague.

If no appointment slot is available, the assistant should offer a callback request and provide the next realistic planning window instead of promising immediate availability.
